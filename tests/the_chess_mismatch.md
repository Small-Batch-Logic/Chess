# The Chess Mismatch: Tron vs. Chess
**Date:** 2026-05-12
**Subject:** Why the current "Intersection" mechanic deviates from the Chess model

If the game is intended to be modelled after **Chess**, the current "Path Intersection" mechanic is actually the wrong inspiration. 

## 1. The Tron Problem
Currently, the game plays more like **Tron** or **Snake**. 
- In Tron, you win by creating a "wall" (a path) that the enemy crashes into.
- In **Board Control**, you win by "drawing" a line that crosses the enemy's "tail." This is a game of **Geometry**, not **Positioning**.

## 2. What makes Chess "Chess"?
To align the game with Chess, the win condition should shift from **Paths** to **Positions**:

| Mechanic | Current (Tron-style) | Chess-style Suggestion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Capture** | Cross their previous path. | Land on the **same stop** they currently occupy. |
| **Check** | Move near their path. | Move within one "hop" of their current stop, threatening a capture. |
| **Checkmate** | N/A (The path just grows). | Trap the System at a terminal or hub where all its exits are "covered" by your pieces. |
| **Pieces** | You have 1 piece that changes type. | You should have **multiple pieces** (e.g., 2 Buses, 1 Subway) on the board at once. |

## 3. The "King" of the Network
In Chess, there is a center of gravity: The King.
- **In Transit Chess:** The System should be a "Ghost Train" (The King) that you are trying to corner.
- **The Accomplishment:** You aren't just crossing its trail; you are **shutting down its options**.

## 4. Multiple Pieces (The "Board" in Chess)
Chess is about the relationship *between* pieces. 
- **The Issue:** Since you only control one "active" bus at a time, there is no "teamwork." 
- **The Fix:** Allow the player to have **3 different vehicles** on the map at once. 
    - You move one per turn. 
    - You win when you use your "Local Bus" to block one exit and your "Subway" to swoop in for the capture. **That is Chess.**

**Conclusion:**
Right now, you've built a beautiful **spatial snake game**. To make it **Chess**, you need to stop focusing on where the System *was* (the trail) and start focusing on where it **is** and where it **can go**.
