# The A-to-B Expectation: Transit vs. Chess
**Date:** 2026-05-12
**Subject:** Why the "Interception" goal is counter-intuitive for a Transit Game

The user's question "Am I trying to get from one side of town to the other?" reveals the **biggest psychological hurdle** for the game:

## 1. The Transit Mental Model
When people see a map with bus icons and stops, their brain immediately switches to "Navigation Mode." 
- **The expectation:** "I am at Stop A, I want to go to Stop B."
- **The reality:** You are at Stop A, and you are trying to "cross" a dashed red line that is moving randomly.

## 2. Narrative Dissonance
The "Chess" part of the brain wants to capture a piece. The "Transit" part of the brain wants to complete a trip. 
- **The Conflict:** By asking the player to "intercept a path," you are asking them to act like a predator (a hunter), but the UI makes them feel like a commuter (a traveler).
- **The Result:** Confusion. "Why am I chasing this red line? I should be trying to get to the airport."

## 3. The "Chess" Label is a Red Herring
Calling it "Chess" suggests a battle of two sides. But in Chess, you move *toward* the enemy's territory. In this game, because the "win" is an intersection, you often win by moving **perpendicular** to the enemy or even away from them to "snip" their trail. This doesn't feel like Chess OR Transit.

---

### How to align the Goal with the Mental Model:
If you want the player to "get it" without reading a manual, the goal should probably be one of these:

1. **The "Race" (The Transit Goal):** You and the System are both trying to reach the same destination. You win if you get there first OR if you intercept them on the way.
2. **The "Blockade" (The Chess Goal):** The System is trying to cross the city. You win by "cutting" all its possible routes so it has nowhere to move (Stalemate = Win).
3. **The "Delivery" (The Logical Goal):** You have 3 "Passengers" (Targets) to pick up. The System is a "Delay" (Enemy) trying to intercept your path. If they intercept you, you lose.

**Final Thought:**
Right now, the game is a **Geometry Puzzle** dressed up as a **Transit Simulator**. The player is looking for the "Destination," but there isn't one.
