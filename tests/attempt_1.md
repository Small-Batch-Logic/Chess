# User Test: Attempt 1 - Discovery
**Date:** 2026-05-12
**Outcome:** Lost (Ghosted)

## Observations
- **Initial Impression:** The "Board Control" title and "Transit Chess" subtitle set a clear theme.
- **Piece Mechanics:** Successfully switched between Local, Express, and Subway. The range visualizer (glowing dots) is helpful but the base network lines are very faint, making it hard to see the "tracks" before moving.
- **Movement:** Moving to a stop is intuitive. The status bar at the top right is necessary to track the AI's rapid moves.
- **Win/Loss:** The "Ghosted" screen appeared when the System intersected my path. The term "Ghosted" is a great thematic choice.

## Issues & Frustrations
1. **Status Lag:** The status text ("System moved to...") seemed to lag slightly behind the marker animation.
2. **Network Visibility:** The grey lines on the map (opacity 0.3) are difficult to see against the light map background, making planning difficult.
3. **AI Speed:** The AI moves quite quickly, which can be jarring if you aren't looking at the right part of the map.
